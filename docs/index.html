The Physics of Play

How fluid dynamics, continuum mechanics, and transport phenomena shape the world’s most popular sport : from the curl of a free kick to the geometry of a defensive block.

I've always been fascinated by how sports are fundamentally shaped by invisible forces: the curl of a free kick, the drag on a long-range shot, the way players position themselves to block passing lanes. This project is my exploration of fluid dynamics through the lens of the sport I love. By building CFD simulations with ΦFlow and SU2, I've been experimenting with how computational physics can reveal the hidden structure in everyday life: from the wake behind a cylinder to the stress fields in a 7v7 formation. It's changed the way I see the world: every flight of a ball, every gust of wind on the pitch, every defensive shift is now a boundary condition waiting to be understood.
