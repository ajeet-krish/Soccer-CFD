Technical Approach

CFD (ΦFlow / JAX)

Incompressible Navier-Stokes on staggered grid. Semi-Lagrangian advection, CG pressure solve.

High-Fidelity (SU2)

Steady/unsteady RANS, SST $k$-$\omega$ turbulence. Unstructured meshes via gmsh. Cl, Cd extraction.

Gaussian Influence

Players as anisotropic Gaussians with velocity stretching. Superposition yields continuous scalar field.

Von Mises Yield

Local maxima in stress field $>$ 0.4 mark defensive breaches. Analogous to structural yield detection.
